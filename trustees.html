⚖️ Fiduciary & Statutory Protection (STSMA Act 8 of 2011)

Trustees hold a statutory fiduciary relationship to the Body Corporate under Section 8(1) of the Sectional Titles Schemes Management Act. Section 8(2) mandates that trustees act honestly, in good faith, and exercise the degree of care, skill, and diligence reasonably expected of persons managing common property.

Why rushing a "quick fix" would have breached fiduciary duty:
Leaving a temporary unanchored friction clamp under 6 bar municipal pressure (which slipped Wednesday night) would have risked catastrophic embankment collapse and undermining the structural foundations of adjacent boundary units. Under Section 8(3), trustees who approve reckless shortcuts face personal liability if damages arise from gross negligence.

Protected by Professional Engineering Standards:
By enforcing SANS 1200 L standards (mass concrete thrust blocks with mandatory 24-hour hydration cure), trustees have fully complied with their legal duty of care. This protects scheme insurance coverage and eliminates repeat repair expenditures.
